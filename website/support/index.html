Something not quite right?

Private support

Email onionary@schaedler.rocks. Include your device, iOS version, app version, and what happened. Never send passwords, passkeys, tokens, or private enrollment links.

Bugs and ideas

Open GitHub issues

Issues are public. Remove personal information and private recipe details before posting.

Connecting to your backend

Enter your server’s HTTPS origin in Settings. This website is the app’s information site, not a recipe backend. The production backend is onionary.malaber.de; development remains at onionary-test.malaber.de.

Passkeys and existing accounts

Use a passkey saved on your device or password manager. Existing accounts without a passkey need an enrollment link from their backend administrator. A cancelled or expired enrollment needs a new link.

Account and data deletion

Your selected backend operator manages your account, recipes, and stored credentials. Contact that operator to request account deletion. For a backend operated by the Onionary developer, email onionary@schaedler.rocks. We will verify ownership before processing requests. Signing out revokes the current session but retains local cooking history. Deleting the app removes its local container under iOS rules; device backups and saved passkeys follow your Apple or password-manager settings.
