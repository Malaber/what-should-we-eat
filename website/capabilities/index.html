Made for cooking

More cooking.

Your recipe book

Search recipes in your active household. Recently visited recipes keep their own progress, so you can return to an unfinished meal.

Portions without rigid steps

Use + and − for whole-portion changes, or type amounts such as 1.53 or 4.32. Have 170 g of the 200 g of cheese called for? Scale every quantified ingredient to match. Cooking times stay unchanged.

A place for every step

Check ingredients and instructions. Go back reverses the last change, including an accidental uncheck. History preserves action timestamps.

Bring your own kitchen

Choose a compatible HTTPS backend and sign in with a passkey. Recipes come from that server. Cooking progress is stored separately for each account on your device.

Honest limits

The companion does not edit recipes or synchronize cooking progress between devices. Use your backend’s web app to manage recipes and households. Previously opened recipes remain available offline.
